## Why We Built Aksara Pro

Khmer script presents unique challenges for digital text editing. Unlike English and many other languages, **Khmer doesn't use spaces between words**. This makes tasks like spell checking, line wrapping, and text selection surprisingly complex.

> "The Khmer script is one of the most beautiful writing systems in the world, but it also presents unique technical challenges for digital tools."

Aksara Pro was built to solve these challenges with modern technology:

1. **Beam Search Word Segmentation** - Our algorithm uses a frequency dictionary of 50,000+ words to intelligently identify word boundaries
2. **SymSpell Spell Checker** - Lightning-fast spell checking with a 77,000+ word dictionary
3. **Voice-to-Text** - Dictate in Khmer with automatic punctuation
4. **ODT Export** - Compatible with LibreOffice for professional documents

## A Quick Example

Here's a simple example of Khmer text that Aksara Pro can handle:

```
សួស្តី! នេះជាឧទាហរណ៍នៃអត្ថបទខ្មែរ។
```

The word breaker automatically segments this into individual words, enabling proper spell checking and line wrapping.
